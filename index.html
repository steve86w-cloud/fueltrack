Nutrition goals

Macro goals do not automatically need to add up to the calorie target. For reference: protein/carbs ≈4 kcal per gram, fat ≈9 kcal per gram.

Your data stays with you

This demo saves entries locally in this browser on this device. No account, subscription or server is needed. Browsers can clear local storage, so export regular backups.

About this version

FuelTrack V5 adds camera barcode scanning and online product lookups via Open Food Facts. Camera scans and product searches need a connection; saved foods and your diary remain available offline. Values are community-contributed: check packaging before logging. No cloud sync or AI coaching is included.

For personal use only, not medical advice. Exercise calorie estimates can be inaccurate and do not automatically increase your daily food goal.
